0=no onboarding concern · 3=learning would require effort · 5=users expected onboarding to be very difficult.
How scoring works

Frequency is based on the share of participants who experienced the issue and is converted to a 0–5 rating. That rating is averaged equally with your ease-of-use, perceived-complexity, and onboarding-impact ratings.

The average becomes a severity level: 4–5 is 1 — Critical, 3–less than 4 is 2 — Major, 2–less than 3 is 3 — Moderate, and below 2 is 4 — Minor. If you select N/A, the tool averages only the available factors.

Foundational insights

Rate the observed contribution of your insight to calculate its impact score.

Use your research notes for context; this tool only calculates impact.

1. How strongly does this insight explain the customer’s ability to accomplish an existing goal or task?
0=no meaningful connection · 3=provides useful understanding · 5=provides critical understanding
2. How strongly does this insight reveal an opportunity to improve task or process efficiency?
0=no efficiency implications · 3=reveals meaningful friction or inefficiency · 5=reveals major friction or inefficiency
3. How strongly does this insight explain what affects customer satisfaction or delight?
0=no meaningful effect · 3=explains a noticeable effect · 5=explains a major effect
How scoring works

Rate how strongly the insight explains a customer’s ability to accomplish a goal, reveals efficiency opportunities, and explains satisfaction or delight. Each applicable dimension is scored from 0 to 5 with equal weight.

If a dimension does not apply, select N/A. The tool averages the available ratings and normalizes the result to a 15-point scale, so an N/A does not lower the score. A score requires at least one applicable dimension.
