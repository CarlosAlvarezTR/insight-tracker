How this score is calculated

Frequency is based on the share of participants who experienced the issue and is converted to a 0–5 rating. That rating is averaged equally with your ease-of-use, perceived-complexity, and onboarding-impact ratings.

The average becomes a severity level: 4–5 is 1 — Critical, 3–less than 4 is 2 — Major, 2–less than 3 is 3 — Moderate, and below 2 is 4 — Minor. If you select N/A, the tool averages only the available factors.
